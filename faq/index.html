Help & FAQ

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Something not working? Most problems are fixed by opening Freeze once. Still stuck? Write to m2brct@gmail.com with your iPhone model and iOS version. We answer every message.

My apps didn't freeze again, or didn't unfreeze

Open Freeze. It checks your snowballs and your time, then freezes or unfreezes your apps again. Freeze relies on Apple's Screen Time, which can sometimes react late, especially just before midnight. If it keeps happening, restart your iPhone, open Freeze, and write to us.

Freeze says Screen Time access is off

iOS can sometimes turn this access off on its own, for example after an update. Open Freeze and allow Screen Time again when asked. You can check it in Freeze › Account. If nothing is asked, go to Settings › Screen Time, and make sure Screen Time is on and Freeze is allowed.

How do I cancel my free trial or subscription? Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Freezium › Cancel Subscription. To end the trial without being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You keep Freezium until the end of the period you paid for, and you then go back to the free plan.

Refunds are decided by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, choose Freeze and "Request a refund".

Where did my snowballs go overnight?

Each night, Frosty keeps 30 snowballs safe and the rest melts. Snowballs earned today never melt tonight. It keeps the game fresh: earn in the day, spend in the day.

What is the emergency joker?

When you have 0 snowballs and no session running, Home shows the emergency joker: your apps open for 3 minutes of use, once a day. It's for the moment you really need an app now.

I froze an app by mistake

New apps freeze right away. An app you remove stays frozen until tomorrow, so you can't skip the game on a whim. Freeze › Account › Frozen apps shows when each one unfreezes. Never freeze an app you need to stay safe, to work or to reach people (alarm, banking, health). iOS always lets you call emergency services.

Can I still open the app's website in Safari?

Freezing an app doesn't always freeze its website. To freeze a website too, add it in Freeze › Account › Frozen apps (on the free plan, the app and the website each count as 1 item).

Do I still get notifications from frozen apps?

That's up to iOS: notifications from a frozen app may be held back or may arrive, depending on the app and the iOS version. If you depend on an app's messages (work, family), don't freeze it, or unfreeze it with a few snowballs to check.

Another app is blocked too (for example Muse when Facebook is frozen)

Some apps sign in through, or are built on, another app's service. When that app is frozen, iOS can block the other one as well. Remove the frozen app from your list (it unfreezes tomorrow), or unfreeze it for a few minutes while you use the other app.

I see a gray "Restricted" screen instead of Freeze's screen

That is iOS's default screen. It appears when iOS couldn't load Freeze's screen in time. Your app is still frozen as it should be. Open Freeze to unfreeze it with snowballs. If it keeps showing, restart your iPhone.
