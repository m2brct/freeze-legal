Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Freeze is made by Amaury de Braucourt. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Freeze on your iPhone.

The short version

  • Freeze has no account, no login, no ads and no tracking.
  • What you enter in Freeze (your name, your answers, your plan) and what you do with it (your frozen apps, your screen time, your steps) stays on your iPhone.
  • Freeze uses Apple's Screen Time to freeze the apps you choose, without ever seeing which apps they are. iOS only tells Freeze when each minute of them is used: that count stays on your iPhone.
  • Only two things leave your iPhone, never with your name, email or Apple ID: whether you subscribe to Freezium (sent to RevenueCat, to unlock it, with the Apple Ads campaign you came from if you installed Freeze from one) and anonymous usage events such as which screens you see (sent to Mixpanel, to improve Freeze). You can turn the usage events off in the Freeze app (Account › Privacy Policy).
  • Delete Freeze and everything it stored on your iPhone is deleted with it. To delete what RevenueCat and Mixpanel keep, email us.

What Freeze stores on your iPhone

To work, Freeze keeps the following on your iPhone only:

  • your first name, if you enter one;
  • your answers during setup: your age, your daily screen time estimate and whether you tried a screen-time app before;
  • your plan: your pace, your weekly targets and your goal;
  • your snowballs, missions, streak and the screen time you traded snowballs for;
  • your settings: appearance, app icon, the start date of your free trial, and which offers or messages were already shown.

This information is stored in Freeze's own storage on your iPhone, shared only with Freeze's own Screen Time extensions. It is never uploaded.

Screen Time

With your permission, Freeze uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) to freeze and thaw the apps and websites you pick. Apple gives Freeze anonymous tokens instead of app names: Freeze cannot see which apps or websites you chose or what you do in them. While they are unfrozen, iOS only tells Freeze when each minute of them is used. Freeze counts these minutes on your iPhone, to spend your snowballs and for your stats. They never leave it. You can turn off Screen Time access at any time in the Settings app.

Missions

Missions only use what they need, on your iPhone, while you do them:

  • Walk counts your steps with your iPhone's motion sensors during the walk. Only the number of steps is used.
  • Keep in Touch reads the name and number of the contact you pick (or the number you type), on your iPhone, only to place the call. They are never stored or sent. Freeze then checks that the call connected and how long it lasted. It never hears the call. After the call, you can share a card with its minutes. Freeze draws it on your iPhone, and it shows no name or number; it only leaves through the share sheet, to where you choose.
  • Study and Brain quizzes are made and checked on your iPhone. Your notes are not uploaded.
  • Breathe only uses a timer.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Freeze schedules them on your iPhone: a reminder a few days before your free trial ends, warnings before your apps freeze, a note when you run low on snowballs, a morning reminder, one when your walk is done, and one that opens Freeze when you tap "Open Freeze" on a frozen app (before iOS 26.5). They are not sent from a server. Turn off the warnings and the morning reminder in Account › Notifications, or all of them in the Settings app.

Purchases

Freezium subscriptions are bought and managed through your Apple ID and the App Store. Apple handles the payment: Freeze never receives your payment details.

To know whether Freezium is active on all your devices, Freeze uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA), a subscription service. It receives your App Store purchase records (product, dates, price, renewal status) under a random ID created by Freeze. With them come the identifier iOS gives Freeze's developer for this iPhone (IDFV, not the advertising identifier) and basic technical details: device model, iOS and app version, language, App Store country. Never your name, email or Apple ID. It is used only to unlock Freezium and restore your purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you installed Freeze from an Apple Ads ad, iOS gives Freeze a token that says which ad campaign it came from (campaign, ad group, keyword, country). It's sent once to RevenueCat with the random ID above, so we can see which ads bring people who subscribe. It contains no advertising identifier, and Apple shares nothing more without your permission, which Freeze never asks for.

Anonymous usage data

To see which screens help and where people give up, Freeze sends anonymous usage events to Mixpanel (Mixpanel, Inc., USA), an analytics service. The events are stored in Mixpanel's European Union data center. For example: which onboarding step you saw, that a paywall was shown, which plan you picked, whether a purchase started, ended or failed, that you changed the app icon or completed a type of mission, that you opened the card after a call and where you shared it (Messages, Instagram… never to whom, and never the card itself), or that you opened the screen to redeem a code.

  • The events are tied to a random ID created by Freeze, never to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier. Freeze doesn't use your IP address to locate you.
  • They never contain your answers, your name, your age, your frozen apps or websites, your screen time, your steps or any health or motion data.
  • Mixpanel also receives basic technical details sent with every event: device model, iOS version, app version, screen size, network type (Wi-Fi or cellular).
  • You can turn this off at any time with the "Share anonymous usage data" switch at the end of this section, in the Freeze app (Account › Privacy Policy). Events not sent yet are deleted.

Mixpanel's privacy policy: mixpanel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

What we share

We do not sell or share any information, and Freeze contains no advertising or tracking code. The only services that receive data are RevenueCat (subscription status, and the Apple Ads campaign if you came from one) and Mixpanel (anonymous usage events), as described above, acting on our behalf. They are based in the United States; data sent to them is protected by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Apple may collect information when you download or buy Freeze, under Apple's own privacy policy. If you chose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They do not identify you.

Keeping and deleting your information

What Freeze stores on your iPhone stays there until you delete Freeze. Deleting the app deletes all of it. Because we never receive it, we cannot see, export or delete it for you: it is on your device, under your control.

Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to manage your subscription and meet legal obligations. Anonymous usage events are kept by Mixpanel for up to 2 years, then deleted. Reinstalling Freeze creates a new random ID.

Email m2brct@gmail.com to have your records deleted from RevenueCat and Mixpanel. To help us find them, include your anonymous IDs, shown in the Freeze app under the "Share anonymous usage data" switch (Account › Privacy Policy).

Children

Freeze is meant for people aged 13 and older. Freeze does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union or the CCPA in California), you have rights over your personal information, such as access, deletion and objection. What Freeze keeps on your iPhone, you control directly: change it in the app or delete the app. You can object to the anonymous usage events by turning them off in the Freeze app (Account › Privacy Policy). For any other request, or to complain to a data protection authority (in France, the CNIL), contact us first and we will help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top, and show the new version in the app.

Contact

When you tap Contact in Freeze, your mail app opens a new email with a few lines at the bottom: the Freeze and iOS versions, your iPhone model, whether Screen Time is allowed, how many apps are frozen, whether they are unfrozen right now and your plan. Nothing is sent until you send it, and you can change or delete those lines first.
