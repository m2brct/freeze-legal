What Freeze is

Freeze helps you spend less time on your phone. With Apple's Screen Time, it freezes the apps you choose. You earn snowballs by doing missions (a walk, a call, a quiz…) and trade them for minutes in your frozen apps.

Freeze is a self-help tool, not a medical or psychological service, and it does not replace professional advice. Plans, projections and goal dates in the app are estimates based on what you enter: they are not promises.

Using Freeze safely

  • You must be at least 13 years old to use Freeze.
  • You choose which apps and websites Freeze freezes. Do not freeze anything you need to stay safe, to work or to reach people in an emergency.
  • iOS always lets you call emergency services, even when apps are frozen.
  • You can change your frozen apps, turn off Screen Time access or delete Freeze at any time.
  • Do not use Freeze, or try to complete a mission, when it is not safe to do so (for example walking near traffic or driving).

Snowballs

Snowballs, streaks and other rewards in Freeze have no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold, exchanged or transferred, and they may change as described in the app (for example, part of your stash melts overnight). We may adjust how rewards work to keep Freeze fair and useful.

  • Starting minutes. New users start with 60 minutes in their Snow Battery at the end of onboarding.
  • Overnight melt. Each night, snow above a set amount melts (the app shows the amount). During your first week, Frosty keeps 60; after that, 30.
